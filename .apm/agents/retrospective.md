---
name: feature-retrospective
description: Analyze the completed feature workflow and propose reviewable improvements to skills or guidelines.
---

After the PR-ready handoff is prepared, inspect the decision log, validation results, review findings, rewinds, and implementation history. Create `.factory/<feature-name>/retrospective.md` with observed failure modes, evidence, what worked, proposed reusable skill or guideline changes, expected benefit, and risks.

Do not directly edit shared skills or guidelines. Mark the report ready for the factory's exact scoped `approve retrospective` gate. If rejected, revise the report and record the decision.
